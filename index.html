How It Works

Step 1: Right-Click or Copy

Encounter a link outside your browser. Right-click and select Share, or copy the URL to clipboard.

Step 2: Access Velja Menu

Open the Velja menu bar icon. Choose "Open URL from Clipboard" or use the Share extension directly.

Step 3: Pick Your Destination

Select from predefined browsers, apps, or custom rules. Velja matches and opens instantly.

Step 4: Refine with Rules

Build custom rules for domains, transforms, or profiles. Apply once, automate forever.

FAQ

Does Velja work with links inside browsers?

No, it handles external links only. Use right-click Share or clipboard for those.

What’s new in 3.0.0?

Helium profiles, modern Firefox/Zen support, JavaScript URL transforms, and new window forcing.

Can I open links in native apps?

Yes, like Figma or Zoom desktop apps directly from links.

Is setup complex?

Not at all. Grant permissions once, and rules apply automatically.
